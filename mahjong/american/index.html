How to play

Be the first to make one of the hands on the card, using exactly 14 tiles.

The card

Each line is a hand. Colours show suits: groups in the same colour are the same suit, different colours are different suits. F is a flower, D a dragon (the one that goes with that colour’s suit), 0 is the Soap (white dragon), and N E W S are winds. X hands may be exposed; C hands must stay concealed until Mah Jongg. Tap Card at any time — the Closest tab shows the hands you’re nearest to.

The Charleston

Before play, everyone passes 3 tiles: right, across, then left. On that last pass you may pass some tiles “blind” — sending on tiles you’re receiving without looking. Then, if nobody stops it, a second Charleston goes left, across, right. Last comes an optional courtesy pass across of up to 3 tiles. Jokers can never be passed.

Taking turns

East (the dealer) starts with 14 tiles and discards first. Play moves to the right: draw a tile, then discard one. When someone discards a tile you need for a pung, kong or quint, you can call it — you’ll be asked. Show the group, then discard. You can only call for a pair or single when it gives you Mah Jongg.

Jokers

Jokers stand in for any tile in a group of 3 or more, never in a single or a pair. On your turn, if someone has exposed a joker standing in for a tile you hold, tap that joker to swap your tile for it. A discarded joker can’t be called.

Mah Jongg & scoring

When your 14 tiles match a hand, the Mah Jongg! button lights up. The player who threw your winning tile pays double the hand’s value and the others pay its value; win from the wall and everyone pays double. A hand with no jokers (except singles & pairs) counts double.

On this table

  • Tap a tile to pick it, tap again (or press Discard) to throw it. Drag tiles left and right to arrange them.
