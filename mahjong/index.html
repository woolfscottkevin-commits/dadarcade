How to play

Clear every tile from the board, two at a time.

Match a pair

Tap two tiles with the same picture. They lift away together.

Only free tiles

A tile is free when nothing sits on top of it and its left or right side is open. Blocked tiles look a little shaded.

Flowers & seasons

Any flower (rose border) matches any other flower. Any season (blue border) matches any other season.

Never stuck

Undo as often as you like. Hint shows a pair that keeps the game winnable. Shuffle re-deals the tiles that are left so the board can still be cleared.

On a keyboard: H hint · Z undo · S shuffle · N new game · Esc menu
