Privacy and support for the Dad Arcade iPhone and iPad app.

Last updated October 18, 2026

What stays on your device

The app stores your favorites, recently played games, and a copy of the game catalog on your device. Games may also save progress and settings locally. These are not sent to an app analytics service. The app has no advertising, subscriptions, or account requirement.

Live games and online scores

The app downloads its current game list and live games from DadArcade.com. These connections send normal technical information to the website and its hosting provider, including your IP address and the requested page. The game player blocks the Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager services used by the general website.

If you choose to submit a leaderboard score, your three-letter initials and score are sent to Dad Arcade and displayed on its public leaderboard. Use initials rather than a full name. Score records include a submission time; IP addresses are used to limit excessive score submissions. Scores are stored through the website’s Upstash service.

Offline copies

The included offline copies of Mahjong Garden, American Mah Jongg, and Balloon Uncle Bop work without connecting to DadArcade.com. Their progress and scores are saved separately on the device. The public balloon game uses illustrated characters.

Your choices

You can clear favorites and recently played history in the app’s About & privacy screen. Deleting the app removes its local data. This does not remove scores already submitted to the online leaderboard. To request removal of a score, contact us with the game, initials, approximate submission time, and score.

Help, content concerns, or privacy requests

Use Report a Glitch on DadArcade.com. Include the game name and what happened. This form also accepts inappropriate-content reports and privacy requests. Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Links opened outside the app have their own privacy behavior. The general Dad Arcade website uses Google Analytics.
