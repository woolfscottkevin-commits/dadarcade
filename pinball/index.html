Field notes

A small guide to the cosmos.

Launch: hold Space (or LAUNCH), then release. Aim for the pulsing top lane for a 1,000-point skill shot.

Flippers: ← / → or A / D. On touchscreens, use the LEFT and RIGHT buttons, or hold either half of the table.

Your expedition: hit the six side stars, then hit the planet bumpers eight times in 30 seconds. Shoot the central eclipse portal to release three-ball multiball. The portal awards jackpots during multiball.

Keep exploring: all three top lanes raise your multiplier. Loop up the left orbit for combos. N nudges the ball, but three quick nudges tilt the table for three seconds. Each new ball gets an eight-second save.

P pauses · M toggles sound. Scores and your personal best cap at 999,999.
